## Shipped gameplay surface in this source

The primary promise is **automatic, biome-styled walls around detected villages on approach**, not a new craftable wall item or a guaranteed wall around every village. `VillageWalls` registers `AutoVillageWallBuilder` and the `WallStyleRegistry`. `AutoVillageWallBuilder` listens for loaded village structures and periodically scans loaded chunks around players; it checks proximity, rolls the common-config `automaticWalls.spawnChance` (default **0.5**, `enabled` defaults true), preloads the search/placement area with bounded budgets, and calls `VillageWallGenerator`. A selected `fortified_beginnings` world-axiom projection can force the automatic path independent of the normal enabled/chance settings (`WorldAxiomPolicy`), but the source-side projection parser is not proof of pack-level identity binding or installed policy. Completed or chance-skipped village cells persist in `better_village_fortifications_processed_villages_v7` SavedData; failures/incomplete search are not automatically proof of completed walls. The generator uses village structures/POIs for the footprint, outlines the enclosure, adapts to terrain and shallow water, selects gate locations and optionally adds lighting for inhabited villages. Large footprints can select a thicker walkable rampart. Specific wall appearance is data-driven; the default style is `temperate`, with selectors in `src/main/resources/data/better_village_fortifications/wall_style_selectors/regions.json`. The op-only player-context `/bettervillagefortifications styles` and `/bettervillagefortifications build <style> <searchRadius> <buffer> <maxDoors>` commands are diagnostic/manual controls, not normal-player acquisition (`VillageWallsCommands`).

**Acquisition/reference contract:** this mod registers **no new item, block, recipe, loot or GUI** in its source. Walls place block states from `src/main/resources/data/better_village_fortifications/wall_styles/*.json`; for example `temperate.json` references vanilla cobblestone, stone bricks, stripped oak logs and wall torches, with optional Quark limestone bricks and Supplementaries sconces. `rampart_stonebrick.json` uses vanilla stone bricks, walls and torches. The block IDs in the palettes are *placed world blocks*, not recipes or grants; normal acquisition is vanilla/other providers' responsibility. `WallStyleRegistry` requires non-optional vanilla palette fallbacks and checks mandatory registered blocks on reload. The only repository-level provider contract is Forge 47+ and Minecraft 1.20.1 on both sides (`mods.toml`); optional palette IDs are not mandatory mod dependencies. There is no `build/ci-provider-jars/providers.json` producer/task in this repository despite the CI upload glob, so that optional artifact must not be reported as provider verification.
